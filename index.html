GeoCraft

Your Minecraft-style world is pinned to real latitude and longitude. One block equals 5 metres. Tap “Use my GPS” to anchor it to where you really are, then enter a destination (or click the minimap) to get live distance, bearing and a walking ETA.

Desktop: WASD move, Space jump, mouse look, left click mine, right click place, 1–5 pick block.
Touch: drag left side to move, right side to look, use the buttons.
