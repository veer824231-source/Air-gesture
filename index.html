Quick Tone Tips

Small rules that make your Mandarin sound far more natural.

The Neutral Tone

Some syllables are short and light with no fixed pitch, like the second 妈 in māma.

Third-Tone Change

When two 3rd tones meet, the first becomes 2nd: nǐ hǎo is spoken as ní hǎo.

The Word 不 (bù)

Before a 4th tone it turns into 2nd tone: bú yào (don't want), bú shì (is not).

The Word 一 (yī)

Before a 4th tone it becomes yí; before 1st, 2nd or 3rd tones it becomes yì.
